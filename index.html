Our story

Two crafts, one welcoming studio

Suvasa Studio started as a small home tailoring business run by Mrs. Chandrika Perera, and has grown into a full salon and dressmaking studio in the heart of Kandy. We believe getting ready for a big day — or just for yourself — should feel personal, unhurried, and genuinely well-made.

Every client is measured, styled, and fitted one-on-one. No rushed appointments, no guesswork — just careful work from people who’ve been doing this for over a decade.

  • Trained & experienced hairstylists and seamstresses
  • Fabric sourcing assistance for custom orders
  • Private fitting room for bridal & occasion wear
